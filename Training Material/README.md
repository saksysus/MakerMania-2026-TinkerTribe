# Design Portfolio

A collection of CAD and digital fabrication projects created using Fusion 360 and LaserCAD.

## Projects

### Bottle Designs
Three bottle concepts designed in Fusion 360, focusing on form, functionality, and manufacturability.

### LaserCAD Designs
Laser-cut projects created for digital fabrication and rapid prototyping.

### Useless Products
A collection of intentionally impractical products designed to explore user perception, design thinking, and creativity.

Current Project:
- Phantom Lock

## Tools Used
- Fusion 360
- LaserCAD
- 3D Printing
- Laser Cutting
