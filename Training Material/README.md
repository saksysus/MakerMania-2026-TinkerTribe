# Tinker Tribe – Design & Fabrication Training Repository

Welcome to the official training repository of **Tinker Tribe**.

This repository documents our learning journey in CAD modeling, laser cutting, digital fabrication, and product design. It contains training exercises, design projects, fabrication files, and experimental concepts developed as part of our hands-on learning process.

## What You'll Find Here

### 🧴 Fusion 360 Bottle Designs
A collection of bottle designs created to practice sketching, 3D modeling, surface design, and product visualization in Fusion 360.

### 🔩 Fusion 360 3D Designs
Various CAD projects designed to strengthen our understanding of modeling techniques, assemblies, design workflows, and manufacturing considerations.

### 🪪 Laser-Cut ID Card Designs
Custom ID card designs created for laser cutting and engraving, focusing on precision, aesthetics, and fabrication readiness.

### 🔑 Laser-Cut Keychain Designs
Creative keychain concepts developed to explore vector design, engraving techniques, and efficient laser-cut manufacturing.

### 💡 Useless Products Project
One of our ongoing design challenges is the creation of **"useless products"**—products that appear useful at first glance but intentionally challenge conventional functionality.

The objective of this project is to:
- Encourage creative thinking
- Explore user perception and assumptions
- Develop storytelling and product-pitching skills
- Experiment with unconventional design ideas

#### Current Concept: Phantom Lock

**"Do people trust a lock because it works, or because it looks like it works?"**

The Phantom Lock is a conceptual product designed to explore how visual appearance influences trust. It resembles a traditional lock in form and presentation, encouraging users to question how often we rely on assumptions rather than verification.

This project combines:
- CAD Design
- Product Storytelling
- Rapid Prototyping
- User Psychology
- Creative Problem Solving

Project files, design iterations, and presentation materials can be found in the **Useless Products** section of this repository.
